Hoot privacy notice

Last updated November 2, 2026

What Hoot is

Hoot helps students learn and study. The Hoot Tutor teaches school courses step by step and helps with homework. A student or teacher can also add review pages, and Hoot makes flashcards and practice tests from them.

What we collect from kids

Nothing that identifies them. Hoot has no student accounts and never asks for a child's name, email, phone number, school or location.

Photos and files

When pages are added, they are sent to our server and then to Claude, an AI service made by Anthropic, to be read. We don't save the photos or files. Anthropic handles them under its commercial terms, which do not allow it to train its models on them. Please photograph school pages only, not faces or personal documents.

Tutor conversations

Messages and homework photos sent to the tutor go to our server and then to Claude to be answered. We don't save them on our server. The tutor is told never to ask for personal information, and to tell a child to talk to a trusted grown-up if they say they are unsafe.

Study sets, chats and scores

A student's study sets, tutor chats, settings and scores are saved only in the browser on their own device. Clearing the browser's data deletes them.

Teacher classes

When a teacher makes a class, we store the class name, the name the teacher chose to show, and the study guides they share (the flashcards and questions, not the original files). A private teacher link controls the class. Students who join with a class code are not recorded.

Grown-up agreement

Before a child uses Hoot on a device, a parent or guardian is asked to read a short notice and agree. That choice is saved only on that device.

Questions or deleting a class

Email robert@vilasi.co. We will answer and delete class data on request.
