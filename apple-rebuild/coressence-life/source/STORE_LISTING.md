**Description:**

Coressence Life is the companion app to two courses by Robert Clinton, MD: Live at Your Peak and Perform at Your Peak. It is not a general tracker. It takes the method the courses teach (assess, plan, do, measure, review, adjust, and keep what works) and runs it with you, one week at a time, using your own data.

YOUR PEAK CYCLE
The home screen is your week on the loop. Six steps laid out as stations on one track show where you are now and what comes next. Under it: this week's course module, today's one to three actions from your plan, and the single biggest signal in your data this week, in plain words.

WEEKLY EXPERIMENT
Each week, pick one change the course teaches, such as bedtime 30 minutes earlier or ten quiet minutes between voice sessions. Mark the days you do it. The app compares the week before with this week from your own sleep, check-in, heart or voice data and gives a plain verdict: keep, adjust or drop, with the reason. What you keep becomes a habit. The rules are written out on the screen.

PERFORMER DAY
For singers, actors and speakers. A countdown to your next show, a voice-load budget for today worked out from your own voice log (and trimmed for hoarseness, a heavy week and the show itself), your warmups with a step-by-step timer, and the performance-day sequence from Perform at Your Peak, with essentials kept apart from optional comforts. Afterwards: one success, one issue, one question for your coach.

WHY THIS NUMBER
Your readiness score, taken apart. See exactly which inputs moved it today and by how many points: heart rate variability and resting heart rate against your own 14-day average, last night's sleep, soreness and overnight temperature. See what was left out, what changed since yesterday, and the whole formula.

THE COURSES
Twelve modules in each course, four performer practice labs, worksheets, weekly homework and a five-step plan builder that turns your course decisions into a calendar. Share a summary with your coach as a file you control, and bring their feedback back in.

THE TOOLKIT
Every tool is filed under the step it serves. Assess: a 20-second check-in, lab results you enter, Apple Health or Health Connect and Bluetooth heart rate straps. Do: guided Pilates, yoga, mobility and strength routines, meals logged by what was on the plate or by barcode, a sleep log with an on-device sleep sound check (no audio is kept), breathing and quiet time, vocal warmups and a voice log. Measure: your numbers against your own average. Review: this week against last, one adjustment, one rule.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your data stays on your phone. No account, no ads, no tracking.

CORESSENCE+
Try free for 14 days. Coressence+ All-Access unlocks every module in both courses, full history with 30- and 90-day trends, every guided routine and patterns across your data. Family plans available. Courses can also be bought once.

Coressence Life is general education and coaching, not medical care, and does not diagnose or treat any condition. Talk with your clinician about your health.
