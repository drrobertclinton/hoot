# Coressence Life Privacy Policy

Effective: October 2026. Publisher: Vilasi, Inc. Contact: support@vilasi.co

## The short version

Everything you enter or measure in Coressence Life stays on your phone. There is no Coressence account, no advertising and no tracking. If you choose to connect a device company's account (such as Oura or Garmin), our connection service passes that company's data to your phone and keeps only an encrypted sign-in token.

## What is stored, and where

Check-ins, habits, workouts, meals, water, eating windows, sleep logs, sleep sound check summaries, recovery and restore logs, voice logs, lab results, plans, calendar items, weekly reviews, lesson answers, device readings and settings are stored in the app's private storage on your device. They are not sent to Vilasi, Inc., Coressence or anyone else.

## Apple Health and Health Connect

If you choose to connect Apple Health (iPhone) or Health Connect (Android), the app reads: steps, distance, exercise minutes, active and resting energy, workouts and the heart rate recorded during them, heart rate, resting and walking heart rate, heart rate variability, sleep and sleep stages, breathing rate, blood oxygen, wrist or skin temperature changes, VO2 max, weight, body fat and lean mass, blood pressure, blood glucose, menstrual flow and period days, mindful minutes, environmental and headphone sound levels (Apple Health), cardio recovery (Apple Health), and ECG classifications and irregular, high and low heart rate notifications (Apple Health), shown exactly as your device reported them.

The app never writes to Apple Health or Health Connect. This data is used only to show your dashboard, trends and notes on your device. It never leaves your phone, is never stored in iCloud, is never used for advertising or marketing, and is never sold or shared. You can remove access at any time in your phone's settings (Health > Data Access on iPhone; Health Connect > App permissions on Android).

## Direct connections to device companies

You can connect Oura, WHOOP, Garmin, Google Health (Fitbit and Pixel Watch), Withings, Polar or Dexcom. You sign in on the company's own page; Coressence never sees your password. Coressence Connect, a small service operated by Vilasi, Inc., stores the access token that company issues, encrypted, and uses it only to fetch your data when your phone syncs and pass it to your phone. Some companies (for example Garmin) send new data to the service as it syncs; that data is stored encrypted only until your phone collects it, and for no more than 7 days. The service keeps no other health data, has no advertising or analytics, and never sells or shares data. Disconnecting in the app deletes the token and anything waiting for delivery, and asks the company to end access where it allows it. Each company's own privacy policy covers the data it holds.

## Bluetooth heart rate straps

During a live workout the app reads heart rate from a Bluetooth strap you pair. Readings stay on your phone and are saved only as part of the workout you log. The app does not use Bluetooth to find your location.

## Microphone (sleep sound check)

The microphone is used only while you run a sleep sound check. The app measures how loud the room is about once a second. The phone needs a temporary recording file while the check runs; it is deleted as soon as the check ends. No audio is kept or uploaded. Only the loudness timeline and summary are saved on your device. The microphone may also pick up other people in the room, so let them know.

## Camera and food lookups (Open Food Facts)

If you choose to scan a food barcode, the camera is used only while the scanner is open, to read the barcode. No photos or video are saved or sent. To look a product up, the app sends the barcode number, or the words you type into food search, to Open Food Facts (openfoodfacts.org), a free open database. Nothing else about you is sent with it. Open Food Facts' own privacy policy covers its servers. The food you log is stored on your device with your other meals.

## Sharing with your coach

If you choose "Share with my coach", the app builds a small summary file from the categories you switch on (each is off until you turn it on), shows you exactly what is in it, and hands it to your phone's share sheet. You decide where it goes; Coressence does not send it anywhere. Coach feedback files you open are stored on your device and can be deleted.

## Reminders

Reminders are scheduled locally on your phone. No push server is involved.

## Purchases

Coressence+ and course purchases are handled by Apple or Google. The app uses RevenueCat to check purchase status with an anonymous identifier. We do not receive your name, email address or payment details.

## Medical services

Coressence Life is general education and coaching, not medical care. Coressence MD and lab panels, when available, are separate services from a licensed medical practice with their own privacy notice. Nothing in the app is shared with them unless you choose to share it.

## Children

The app is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13.

## Deleting your data

Settings > Delete all my data removes everything the app stored on your device. Deleting the app also removes it. To remove device connections from Coressence Connect as well, disconnect each one in Connected devices first, or email support@vilasi.co. Purchases stay with your Apple or Google account and can be restored.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted at the privacy link in the app with a new effective date.
