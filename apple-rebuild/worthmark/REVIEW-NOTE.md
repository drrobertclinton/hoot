# Worthmark: note for App Review

Worthmark is a mindful-buying notebook for adults. It helps decide whether something is worth buying, and checks afterwards whether it was. It was written from scratch and shares no layout, code, styling or navigation with any other app from this developer.

What makes it distinct:

- A single typeset "ledger" of dated entries instead of tabs and cards. Entries open as full pages. There is no tab bar.
- The Worth Test: a seven-question interview that ends in a BUY, WAIT or SKIP verdict with written reasons.
- Cool-off Vault: a 24-hour, 72-hour or 7-day live countdown, after which the app asks "still want it?" and totals the money kept by skipping.
- Cost per use: price divided by expected uses, compared with alternatives the user enters; after buying, one-tap tally marks show the real cost per use falling.
- Regret check: 30 days after a purchase the user rates it 1 to 5; Insights show hit rate and most-regretted categories.
- A monthly "wants" budget guardrail.

Price-check buttons open ordinary shop searches in the browser. There are no affiliate links, purchases, accounts, ads or tracking. Data stays on the device; Settings offers JSON export and delete-all.
