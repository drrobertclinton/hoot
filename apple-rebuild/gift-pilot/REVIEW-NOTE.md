# Shop Gift Pilot — note for App Review

Gift Pilot is a gift planner for adults who buy for many people. It was written from scratch and shares no code, layout or artwork with other Vilasi, Inc. apps.

What is specific to this app:

- An airport "Departures board" home screen. Each row is a person's upcoming occasion with a split-flap countdown and a five-step status (Planning, Gift chosen, Bought, Wrapped, Delivered). Navigation is a three-way switch at the top of the board; there is no bottom tab bar.
- Each person has a boarding-pass card holding occasions, notes, a wish list with secret "bought" marks, and gift history that warns before a repeat.
- Gift Interview: a swipe deck of yes/no cards plus a budget slider produces three picks, each with a stated reason, from 186 gift ideas bundled in the app.
- Occasions radar with reminder lead times and .ics calendar export.
- Season budget Logbook comparing planned and actual spend per person.

No account, analytics or in-app purchases. Data stays on the device, with JSON export and delete-all in Settings. "Find it" opens an external web search. An 18+ note appears on first launch; wine and spirits ideas stay hidden until the user confirms 21+.
