# Jonah Activity Club — note for App Review

Jonah Activity Club is a learning-and-play app for children aged 3–11 (Kids category), built around the characters of the Jonah Pruitt picture books: Jonah, his friends Dez, Priya and Tuck, and his dog Pickles.

It was designed and coded from scratch and shares no layout, navigation, artwork or code with our other apps:

- Navigation is a scrolling, hand-drawn island map, not tabs or lists. A child taps a place and Pickles walks the path there before it opens.
- Six working activities: coloring (tap-to-fill plus free crayon), letter and number tracing with stroke checking, counting and memory match, generated mazes, word matching and word search, and a sticker scene with drag, scale and rotate.
- The Pickles a child colors becomes the Pickles that walks the map and runs the mazes.
- Spoken prompts mean ages 3–5 need no reading; three age bands change difficulty.
- All artwork is original SVG drawn for this app.

A parent gate (3-second hold plus a multiplication) protects settings, progress, printing, export and delete. The app has no ads, accounts, analytics, purchases, external links or network access; all data stays on the device.
