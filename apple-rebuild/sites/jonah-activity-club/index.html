An island full of things to do.

Jonah Activity Club is a seaside island that children explore by tapping. Pickles the dog walks the path to each place, and every place is an activity from the Jonah Pruitt picture books.

Coming to the App StoreFor iPhone and iPad.

The island

No menus. Just a map.

Tap the treehouse, the tide pools, the dock, the boardwalk or the lighthouse and Pickles trots there. Spoken prompts mean children aged 3 to 5 do not need to read.

Color Pickles

The dog you color is the dog who walks

Tap to fill or draw with a free crayon. The Pickles a child colors becomes the Pickles on the map and in the mazes.

  • Coloring pages with tap-to-fill and crayons
  • Letter and number tracing that checks each stroke
  • Counting, memory match, mazes, word hunts and a sticker treehouse

For grown-ups

Three age bands and a parent gate

Choose ages 3 to 5, 6 to 8 or 9 to 11 and the activities change difficulty. Settings, progress, printing and delete sit behind a press-and-hold gate with a sum.

Safe by design

No ads

No ads, no purchases and no links out of the app.

No accounts

Nothing to sign up for. No names, emails or photos are collected.

No internet

The app works fully offline. Progress stays on the device.
