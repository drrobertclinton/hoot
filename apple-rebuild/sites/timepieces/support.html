Support

Need help with Vilasi Timepieces, or found something that looks wrong? Email robert@vilasigrowth.com and tell us which iPhone or iPad you use. We reply within two business days.

How does true-scale Wrist Fit work?

Hold a bank card against the screen once to calibrate. After that, watches are drawn at their real size in millimetres.

How accurate is the timegrapher?

It measures beat rate, rate and beat error from the sound of the movement. A quiet room and the phone’s microphone near the case back give the best reading. It is a guide, not a replacement for a watchmaker’s instrument.

Where is my collection stored?

On your phone only. Use the export in Workshop to keep a backup or an insurance summary.

Is this connected to a watch brand?

No. Vilasi Timepieces is independent and sells nothing.
