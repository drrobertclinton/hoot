Support

Need help with Cellar & Table, or found something that looks wrong? Email robert@vilasigrowth.com and tell us which iPhone or iPad you use. We reply within two business days.

Where is my cellar stored?

On your phone. Nothing is uploaded, so it does not sync between devices. Use Export a backup in Settings to move it or keep a copy.

How are drinking windows worked out?

From the wine type and vintage, using rules you can edit in Settings. You can also set the years for a single bottle. They are guidance, not a guarantee.

Can I have more than one rack?

Yes. Add racks, fridges or cases under Racks & Fridges, each with its own rows and columns.

Does it sell wine?

No. Cellar & Table has no shop, no purchases and no ads.
