Your week on one loop.

Coressence Life is the companion app to Live at Your Peak and Perform at Your Peak by Robert Clinton, MD. It runs the method the courses teach, one week at a time, with your own data.

Coming to the App StoreFor iPhone.

Your Peak Cycle

Assess, plan, do, measure, review, adjust

The home screen is your week laid out as six stations on one track. It shows where you are now, this week’s lesson, today’s one to three actions and the biggest signal in your data.

Weekly Experiment

One change a week, judged by your own numbers

Pick one change the course teaches, such as bedtime 30 minutes earlier. Mark the days you do it. The app compares the week before with this week and gives a plain verdict: keep, adjust or drop.

Performer Day

A show-day mode for singers, actors and speakers

A countdown to your next show, a voice-load budget worked out from your own voice log, warmups with a timer, and the performance-day sequence from Perform at Your Peak.

Why this number

Your readiness score, taken apart

See which inputs moved the score today and by how many points: heart rate variability, resting heart rate, sleep and soreness, each against your own average.

  • Reads Apple Health; never writes to it
  • Share a summary with your coach as a file you control

Private by design

On your phone

No Coressence account, no ads, no tracking. What you enter and measure stays on your device.

Your health data

Apple Health is read-only and is never used for advertising or shared.

Education, not care

General education and coaching. Talk with your clinician about your health.
