Privacy

Effective 14 November 2026. Shop Gift Pilot is published by Vilasi, Inc.

The short version

Shop Gift Pilot does not collect, send or sell your personal information. What you enter stays on your device.

What the app stores

Everything you add is saved in the app’s own storage on your phone or tablet. There is no account and no server copy. If you delete the app, that data is deleted with it, so export a backup first if you want to keep it.

“Find it” opens a web search for a gift idea in your browser. Some links may earn Vilasi, Inc. a commission at no cost to you. The app does not see what you do on those sites.

What the app does not do

No advertising, no analytics, no tracking across apps or websites, and no sharing of data with third parties.

Your choices

You can export your data as a file or delete all of it at any time from the app’s settings.

Children

The app is made for adults and is not directed at children.

This website

This website, unlike the app, may record basic visit statistics such as pages viewed and approximate region, through our website host. It does not know who you are in the app, and the app does not talk to this website.
