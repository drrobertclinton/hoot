Things worth having, and things worth leaving.

Worthmark is a ledger for the things you want to buy. It slows the decision down just enough to tell a real want from an impulse, then checks afterwards whether you were right.

Coming to the App StoreFor iPhone.

§ I · The cool-off vault

Set it aside for 24 hours, 72 hours or a week

The vault counts down to the second. When it opens, Worthmark asks one question: still want it? Say no and the price is added to the money you kept.

§ II · The Worth Test

Seven questions, then a verdict

Do you own something that does this already? Will you use it next week? The test ends in BUY, WAIT or SKIP, with the reasons written out so you can argue with them.

§ III · Cost per use

What each use really costs

Price divided by the uses you expect, set beside the cheaper pair, the rental or keeping what you have. After you buy, one tap per use shows the real figure coming down.

  • Insights: your hit rate and your most regretted categories
