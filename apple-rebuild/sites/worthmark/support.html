Support

Need help with Worthmark, or found something that looks wrong? Email robert@vilasigrowth.com and tell us which iPhone or iPad you use. We reply within two business days.

Is this a budgeting app?

No. It does not connect to a bank. It keeps a monthly “wants” allowance you set yourself and a record of each decision.

What happens when the vault opens?

You are asked whether you still want the item. Yes moves it back to deciding; no records it as skipped and adds the price to money kept.

Where is my ledger stored?

On your phone only. Export a backup from Settings.

Does Worthmark earn money when I buy?

No. There are no affiliate links or purchases.
