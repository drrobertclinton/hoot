Cellar & Table is a wine cellar manager and dinner-pairing tool for adults. It asks for a one-time 21+ confirmation on first launch.

It was rebuilt from scratch with its own code, layout and artwork, and shares no shell, tab bar, card list or palette with any other app from this developer.

- The home screen is a mirror of the user's physical wine rack: a grid of slots sized to their real rack. Bottles appear as coloured capsule tops that glow when the wine is in its drinking window. Long-press and drag moves bottles; search lights up matching slots.
- A horizontal year timeline shows every bottle's drinking window, grouped as open now, hold and past peak, with editable rules.
- A rotating pairing wheel of 40 dishes suggests wine styles and highlights matching bottles the user owns.
- Tasting notes use a tappable aroma wheel, 100-point or 5-star scores, and move finished bottles to a journal.
- Label photos, spend and value statistics.

Tools live in a pull-up drawer, not a tab bar. No accounts, purchases, ads, analytics or network use. Data stays on the device, with JSON export and delete-all in Settings.
