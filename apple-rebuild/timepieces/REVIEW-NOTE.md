# Vilasi Timepieces — note for App Review

Vilasi Timepieces (formerly Watch Genie) is a tool for adults who own or are buying wristwatches. It was rebuilt from scratch and shares no layout, code or navigation with other Vilasi, Inc. apps.

There is no tab bar. The home screen is a live watch dial drawn from each watch's own attributes, with hands showing the real time. Users swipe between watches and tap segments of the surrounding bezel to open tools:

- Wrist Fit: draws a watch at true physical scale on the screen (calibrated with a bank card), with a fit verdict.
- Accuracy Log: tap-to-sync timing against the phone clock, rate chart in seconds per day, and alerts when a movement drifts beyond its specification (for example COSC).
- Timegrapher: uses the microphone to hear a mechanical movement and measure beat rate, rate and beat error. Audio is analysed live and never recorded or stored.
- Rotation: wear log, calendar heatmap and suggestions.
- Service & Papers: service dates, warranty, box-and-papers checklist, insurance summary export.
- Finder: six questions against a bundled reference set of 46 models.

No account, analytics, ads or purchases. All data stays on the device.
