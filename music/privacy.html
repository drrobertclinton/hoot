Privacy Policy

Effective November 2, 2026

Vilasi Music is published by Vilasi, Inc., a North Carolina corporation (“we,” “us”), 1475 Midland Road, Unit 41, Southern Pines, NC 28387. This policy explains what happens to information in the app. Questions are welcome at robert@vilasigrowth.com.

Your progress stays on your device

Your lesson progress, stars, XP, streak, practice log, flashcards, practice plan, optional first name and settings are stored only on the device you are using. They are not uploaded to us and we do not have a copy. Vilasi Music has no account, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Because we never receive this information, we cannot recover it for you. Use Settings, Back up to save a copy in a file you control, and Restore to bring it back.

Microphone

The app asks for the microphone only when a lesson, song, tuner or other exercise needs to hear you, and only after you press Start. The sound is analyzed on the device, moment by moment, to work out which notes, chords or claps you played and how in tune they were. Nothing is recorded, saved or sent anywhere, and listening stops when you leave the exercise.

MIDI keyboards

If you connect a MIDI keyboard, the app reads which keys you press and how hard, on the device, to score your playing. Nothing about the keyboard or your playing leaves the device.

Sharing reports

If you choose Share weekly report or Export CSV, the app prepares the text or file on your device and hands it to your device’s share sheet. You decide where it goes. We do not receive it.

Purchases

If you buy a subscription or the one-time Pro purchase, payment is handled by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). We never see your card details. To know what you have bought on any of your devices, the app uses RevenueCat, a service that keeps a record of your purchases (product, date, store receipt) linked to a random app user ID. It does not include your name or email, and it is not used for advertising or tracking.

Your choices

  • Erase everything the app has stored on your device in Settings, Erase everything.
  • Turn microphone access off in your device settings at any time. Lessons still work with the on-screen keys.
  • Ask us anything about this policy, or about purchase records held by RevenueCat for us, by email.

Children and families

Vilasi Music is designed for teens and adults and is suitable for younger students practicing with a parent or teacher. We do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13: the app has no sign-up and sends nothing about its users to us.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will post the new version here with a new effective date.
