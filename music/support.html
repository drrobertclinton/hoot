Help

Answers to common questions. For anything else, email robert@vilasigrowth.com and we will reply within two business days.

Listening and scoring

The app does not hear my notes

Allow the microphone when asked (or turn it on in your device settings under Vilasi Music). Play one clear note at a time, a little louder than usual, close to the phone or tablet. Quiet rooms work best. The “Hearing …” label under the music shows what the app is picking up.

The band is confusing the scoring

Wear wired headphones so the backing band does not reach the microphone, or switch the Band toggle off. Bluetooth headphones add delay; if you use them, set the timing correction in Settings.

It says I am late when I am on the beat

Every device adds a small delay between sound and analysis. Open Settings, Timing correction, Measure it and clap along with eight clicks. The app sets the correction for you.

Can I use a MIDI keyboard?

In the web version (Chrome, Edge or Opera on a computer or Android), yes. Connect it by USB or Bluetooth MIDI before opening a lesson. The app finds it automatically and scores every key, including chords. Choose MIDI keyboard in Settings if you want it to ignore the microphone. In the iPhone, iPad and Android apps, use the microphone: it works with any piano or keyboard.

Can I practice silently?

Yes. Tap On-screen keys in any play-along to answer with the on-screen keyboard.

Lessons and progress

What is free?

The first unit of every instrument course, the first two units of music theory, five songs, the metronome, tuner and drone, and the practice log, streaks and achievements. Pro opens everything else.

How do streaks and freezes work?

Practice on consecutive days to grow your streak. You earn a streak freeze every seven days (up to two); a freeze covers one missed day automatically.

How do I move my progress to a new phone?

Your progress stays on your device. Use Settings, Back up to save a file, move it to the new device, and choose Restore.

Sharing practice with a teacher or parent

Open Me, Practice log. Share a weekly report as text or export the full log as a spreadsheet file.

Purchases

Restore a purchase

Open Settings, Membership, Restore Purchases while signed in to the same Apple or Google account you used to buy.

Cancel a subscription

On iPhone or iPad: Settings, your name, Subscriptions. On Android: Play Store, Payments and subscriptions, Subscriptions. Pro stays active until the end of the period you paid for.
